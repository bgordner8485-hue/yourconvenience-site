Why we put every winning ticket on the wall

Walk into our store in South Williamsport and you'll see photos of winning tickets. Not lottery-office press photos — actual tickets sold at our counter, scratched at our counter, and paid out at our counter.

We started taking those pictures for a simple reason: people want to know a store pays. Now every one of them lands on our winners wall automatically, with the amount and the date, usually within minutes of the ticket being cashed.

What the wall tells you

Scroll it and you'll notice patterns. Certain games produce steady $100 and $150 hits. Others go quiet for weeks and then drop a $500 or $850. It's not a system — the Lottery's odds are the Lottery's odds — but it does tell you which games are actually moving in this rack, in this town.

Every Sunday we take that week's winners, set them to one of our own songs, and post the montage. If your ticket is up there, that's your ticket in the video.

The part we can't promise

We can show you what's been sold. We can't tell you what's coming. Anybody who says otherwise is selling something. Play what you enjoy, spend what you can afford to lose, and if it stops being fun, stop — the number for free, confidential help in Pennsylvania is 1-800-GAMBLER.

Meanwhile: today's numbers, top prizes left on every scratch-off, and the wall itself. Stop in and add to it.
