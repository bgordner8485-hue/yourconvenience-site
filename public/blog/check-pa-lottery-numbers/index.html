The fastest way to check your PA Lottery numbers

Most people check their ticket one of two ways: squint at the monitor above the counter, or dig through the Lottery's site game by game. We built a third way.

One page, every game

Our winning numbers page lists the latest drawing for every Pennsylvania game in one place — PICK 2, PICK 3, PICK 4 and PICK 5 (day and evening, with the Wild Ball), Cash 5, Match 6, Treasure Hunt, Millionaire for Life, Powerball and Mega Millions. It refreshes straight from the Lottery's own feed shortly after each drawing, so you don't have to click into six different games to check three tickets.

Each game also has its own page with the past 120 drawings — handy if you play the same numbers and want to see how they've run: PICK 3, PICK 4, Cash 5, Match 6, Powerball.

When your ticket wins

  • Under $600: bring it to our counter. We cash winners in the store, and that's true of both our locations.
  • $600 and up: those get claimed through the Lottery, by mail or at a Lottery office. Sign the back of the ticket first — an unsigned ticket belongs to whoever is holding it.
  • Always double-check. What we post here is for convenience and is unofficial. The Lottery's own results are the ones that count.

Then see what's been hitting

While you're checking, look at the winners wall — every winning ticket we've sold, with the amount. A lot of people come in after seeing a $500 or $1,000 ticket go out the door on a game they hadn't tried yet. And if scratch-offs are your thing, our top prizes remaining page shows exactly how many of the big prizes are still out there on each game before you spend your money.
